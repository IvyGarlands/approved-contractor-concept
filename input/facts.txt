CONFLICT #1 — PHONE NUMBER
  Homepage header shows (866) 215-2968.  [P] homepage, tel:866-215-2968
  Contact page, About page and the CSLB register all show (877) 792-9082.
  RESOLUTION: the state register is authoritative and agrees with two of their
  own pages; the 866 number behaves like a call-tracking line. (877) 792-9082 is
  used. The 866 number is not used anywhere. Worth raising with them — a header
  number that disagrees with their licence record is a real trust leak.

CONFLICT #2 — SAN DIEGO ADDRESS
  Their own San Diego page publishes the Canoga Park address and phone.
  BBB lists 5173 Waring Rd #86, San Diego CA 92120 / (619) 933-8306.
  Houzz lists Tarzana, CA 91356.
  RESOLUTION: three sources, three answers, only one of them theirs. Only the
  Canoga Park address on the CSLB register is used. "Two locations in Los
  Angeles and San Diego" is quoted as their claim without a second address.

CONFLICT #3 — JAMES HARDIE DESIGNATION WORDING
  Homepage badge: "james hardie elite contractor".
  San Diego page: "James Hardie Elite Alliance Contractor".
  RESOLUTION: both are theirs and they differ only in wording. The shorter form
  is used, attributed to them. Not independently verified — jameshardie.com is
  behind a bot challenge which I did not attempt to bypass.

CONFLICT #4 — AGGREGATE RATING AND REVIEW COUNT  (the big one)
  Their own site:        "Based on over 800+ reviews" and "nearly 800 5-star
                          reviews"  [P] homepage
  GuildQuality:          4.6 stars, 85 reviews from 99 respondents of 314
                          surveys sent; "75 of 84 would recommend"  [T]
  Houzz:                 4.7 out of 5, 77 reviews  [T]
  Yelp:                  125 reviews  [T] (count only; page behind bot
                          verification, not bypassed)
  authenticfeedback:     5.0, 258 reviews  [T] — and demonstrably contaminated,
                          see CONFLICT #5
  RESOLUTION: no aggregate rating, no star count and no review total appears
  anywhere in the build, and no aggregateRating is emitted in the JSON-LD. The
  numbers are irreconcilable and every one of them is a different denominator.
  The page carries individually attributed reviews instead. This is a deliberate
  and defensible omission: on a page whose entire argument is "check us", an
  unverifiable "800+ 5-star reviews" is the one thing on it that would not
  survive being checked.

CONFLICT #5 — CONTAMINATED REVIEW AGGREGATOR
  reputation.authenticfeedback.com attributes a review to this business that
  reads "Choosing Line of Art Construction for our driveway..." — a review of a
  different company, for a service they do not offer.
  RESOLUTION: the whole source is treated as unreliable. Four Google reviews it
  surfaced are logged in reviews.txt but NOT published. See reviews.txt §3.

CONFLICT #6 — HOURS
  No primary source publishes hours. Third-party listings disagree on Saturday.
  RESOLUTION: omitted entirely. See §3.
