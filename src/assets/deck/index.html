Why it matters

Three things a working site would do for you

  1. 01

    Answer the price question before it costs you a call

    Right now your homepage lists memberships at $149, $249 and $399, and your memberships page lists $249, $399 and $599. Someone comparing the two gets two answers. One clear set of prices, in one place, is the whole fix.

  2. 02

    Look as credible online as you already are in the room

    Your reviews say welcomed, unhurried, never pushed. Your site opens with a page title that still reads "Setiba Medical Spa V2", and every link you share shows an arrow icon instead of your work. The room is better than the website.

  3. 03

    Put all forty-three treatments where people can find them

    Injectables through to hormone therapy, in six clear categories, each with what it is for. Plus the thing your site never says out loud: that everything happens under a named Medical Director.

Every point above comes from your own site, your own Yelp reviews, or your own listings. Nothing here is a statistic I cannot show you.
